Troubleshooting Guide

            

            

              

                Why does NPU show 'Absent on x86_64'?
                
NEXUS truthfully detects whether Qualcomm's Hexagon NPU is physically present. When running on standard AMD/Intel PCs, it falls back to CPU execution without falsifying NPU results.

              

              

                How does Voice Input work without internet?
                
Voice dictation is decoded on your local CPU/NPU using OpenAI Whisper Small Quantized. No audio data ever leaves your device.
